04 / Methodology

Understand the model behind the numbers.

Built from the processed 2026 Stack Overflow Developer Survey as part of the University of Toronto's MIE1624 Assignment 2.

01

Ordinal classification

Fourteen L1-regularized cumulative-threshold logistic regressions predict 15 ordered salary buckets. Cumulative outputs are made monotonic, then converted into normalized bucket probabilities.

02

Original input, fitted transforms

Questions use survey responses, never manually entered encoded indicators. Missing values, numerical scaling, one-hot and multi-hot features use statistics and vocabularies fitted only on the training set.

03

What performance means

The model achieved 1.3179 bucket MAE and 32.53% accuracy on 2,702 held-out respondents. Rare and extreme salary buckets are difficult to predict; results are exploratory.
